# Code of Conduct

TokenPoints is a small open project. We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), distilled to what actually matters here.

## What we expect

- **Be useful.** If you're commenting, contributing, or critiquing, do it in a way that helps someone — the maintainer, another contributor, a future reader.
- **Be honest.** Disagreement is welcome; framework dogma isn't. Tell us when something doesn't work for your team and why. That's more valuable than another success story.
- **Be specific.** Vague opinions get vague responses. Bring data, examples, or concrete proposals when you can.
- **Be kind.** Personal attacks, harassment, and bad-faith engagement are not welcome. We don't have rules for what counts as "bad faith" — if you have to ask, you're probably already past the line.

## What you can expect from maintainers

- **Quick acknowledgment.** Issues and PRs get a response within a few days, even if the response is "I need more time to look at this."
- **Honest review.** If your contribution doesn't fit, you'll get a clear explanation, not silence.
- **Credit where it's due.** Translators, contributors, and people who shaped the framework get named in the README.

## Reporting issues

If someone is behaving in a way that violates this code:

1. **Open a private issue or DM the maintainer** — preferred for most situations. Find current maintainers in the repo's [contributors page](https://github.com/rogerio-grocers/tokenpoints/graphs/contributors).
2. **Public issue** — only if the behavior is already public and warrants public response.
3. **Email** — if you'd rather report privately and don't want to use GitHub channels, send to the email listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

Maintainers will respond within 7 days.

## Enforcement

Violations will be addressed at the maintainer's discretion. Possible responses, in roughly increasing order:

1. Private reminder of the expectations.
2. Public warning on the relevant thread.
3. Temporary block from the repo (e.g., 30 days).
4. Permanent block.

We try to use the lightest response that solves the problem. We also reserve the right to skip steps for severe violations (harassment, threats, doxxing).

---

*This Code of Conduct applies to all repository spaces — issues, PRs, discussions, and any external community channels (Discord, Slack, etc.) that may exist now or in the future.*
